# PRESENSE DESIGN IDENTITY — THE SIGNATURE STYLE

The app currently lacks a signature style. Every design decision below creates one consistent identity called: **Warm Atmosphere**. 

The philosophy is this: Presense feels like a warm lamp in a dark room. In dark mode it glows from within against deep space. In light mode it breathes warm morning light — cream and amber, never clinical white.

The four pillars that never change regardless of theme or mode:

### Pillar 1 — Atmosphere over flatness
No surface is ever plain. Every background has ambient light from orbs, every card has a surface shimmer, every modal has grain. The app always feels like it exists in an environment, not on a blank canvas.

### Pillar 2 — Warmth at the centre
The accent family is always warm: amber, coral, deep orange. Cool colours (teal, blue) appear only as secondary accents and space identifiers. The default experience is warm.

### Pillar 3 — Glass as the language of depth
Cards, panels, modals, dropdowns, toasts — everything is a glass surface floating in the atmospheric background. The glass recipe changes between dark mode (more opaque, darker tint) and light mode (lighter frosted tint with the background bleeding through more) but the glass concept never disappears.

### Pillar 4 — Inter carries the voice
All type is Inter. No mixing fonts except JetBrains Mono for numeric data. Type is confident — large where it matters, restrained where it supports.
